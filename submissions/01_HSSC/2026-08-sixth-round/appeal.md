Dear Editor,

We appreciate the time and care the reviewers invested throughout the review process. We recognise that the manuscript has limitations, and we are committed to revising it further.

We agree that several of Reviewer 4’s remaining points are worth addressing directly in the manuscript, and we would be pleased to make those revisions.

At the same time, we respectfully request clarification regarding the scope of a further revision. We would be grateful if the next round could remain focused on the substantive concerns that have already been identified during the review process, rather than requiring a fundamental rewriting of the manuscript or the development of a substantially different study.

We fully acknowledge that the study does not address every possible question concerning AI-supported learning, and we have revised the manuscript to avoid overstating its contribution. We also recognise that some of Reviewer 4’s remaining concerns can be addressed constructively through further clarification, refinement of the theoretical framing, and more cautious presentation of the findings, and we are willing to make those changes.
Our concern is primarily with the scope of the requested changes. In particular, the suggestion that the manuscript should be “rewritten from scratch, focusing primarily on Experiment 2” would fundamentally alter the research questions, theoretical framing, presentation of the results, and intended contribution. Such a change would go beyond revising the manuscript in response to the concerns raised in the review process and would effectively require the development of a substantially different manuscript.

We therefore respectfully ask whether the further round could instead be scoped to the concerns already on record and revisions that can reasonably be made within the existing study, data, research questions, and overall contribution. We believe this would allow us to respond directly and constructively to the remaining substantive issues while preserving the integrity and purpose of the original manuscript.
We would also be grateful for editorial adjudication after we submit the revised manuscript. Given that two reviewers have recommended publication following revision, we hope that, once we have addressed the remaining concerns that can reasonably be resolved through revision, the manuscript can be evaluated based on the revised submission and the full set of reviewer assessments.

We understand that Reviewer 4 may have alternative preferences regarding how the manuscript should be framed or which aspects should receive greater emphasis. However, where such preferences do not identify a fundamental flaw in the study or evidence, we respectfully hope that they would not, by themselves, constitute a barrier to acceptance.

We emphasise that our intention is not to challenge the value of Reviewer 4’s feedback. The review has substantially helped us improve the manuscript, and we are committed to addressing the points that can strengthen it. Rather, we seek guidance on whether the remaining round can be understood as a revision of the manuscript currently under review, focused on the concerns already raised, rather than as a request for a fundamentally different paper.
Thank you again for your consideration and for the opportunity to improve the manuscript.

Sincerely,
