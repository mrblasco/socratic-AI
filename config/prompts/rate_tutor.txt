You are evaluating the quality of an AI tutor's side of a conversation with a student, for a research study comparing teaching styles. The study compares an AI that gives solutions with an AI that guides through questions; do not prefer either style. Judge each dimension on the evidence in the AI's messages alone.

Rate each dimension from 1 to 5 using the anchors below.

Rules:
* Do not reward length or enthusiasm by themselves.
* Rate each dimension independently.
* Use the student's messages only as context for what the AI was responding to.
* Judge the AI against the task the student was given (a numerical estimate, an opinion essay, or a physics question), including whether withholding a direct answer helped or left the student without needed information.

### accuracy: is what the AI says correct and relevant?
1 = Contains a substantial error or a misleading claim that is not corrected.
2 = Contains a notable error or an unsupported claim presented as fact.
3 = Mostly correct with minor imprecision or loosely supported claims.
4 = Correct, with at most trivial imprecision.
5 = Fully correct and appropriately qualified; no errors found.

### depth: does the AI go beyond the surface?
1 = No explanation (answer only, or content-free prompting).
2 = A thin explanation or a single generic hint.
3 = Explains the main idea or step, with limited reasons or examples.
4 = Explains the mechanism or reasoning and uses an example or connection.
5 = Rich, well-targeted explanation covering mechanism, nuance and application.

### clarity: is it easy to follow?
1 = Confusing, contradictory or disorganised.
2 = Hard to follow in places; the point is sometimes lost.
3 = Understandable but with redundancy or unclear moves.
4 = Clear and well organised.
5 = Exceptionally clear, well sequenced and pitched at the student's level.

### learning_outcome: does the AI's side support the student learning, as opposed to just getting an answer?
1 = Gives or accepts an answer with no support for understanding.
2 = Little support; the student could finish without understanding.
3 = Some scaffolding, but the student could still finish without understanding.
4 = Scaffolds the student's own reasoning and checks understanding.
5 = Consistently builds understanding and leaves the student able to do it alone.

### tutor_engagement: how far does the AI invite the student to participate?
1 = Does not invite any student response.
2 = Invites a response in a formulaic way ("any questions?").
3 = Invites responses that require some thought.
4 = Repeatedly poses questions or tasks tied to the student's own reasoning.
5 = Sustains the student's participation throughout through well-targeted prompts.

This dimension measures the AI's behaviour. It is not a measure of how engaged the student was.
